## Detailed description

DualCoach is the dual-subtitle companion to **Coach de lecture française**.

Watch YouTube or Netflix with two subtitle lines at once — the language you’re learning on top, your language underneath. Click any word for a definition, hear it spoken, and save it to your vocabulary list.

- Dual subtitles synchronized with playback  
- Transcript sidebar with the current line highlighted  
- Local vocabulary database (export CSV / Anki)  
- Optional AI explanations via **OpenRouter free models**, Gemini, or Anthropic  
- Cream & forest-green UI matching the reading coach  

Not affiliated with Netflix or YouTube.
