General

On other sites DualCoach looks for HTML5 <video> plus subtitle tracks or linked .vtt/.srt files (Archive.org-style, Video.js, Plyr, MediaElement, and similar open players). Sites that only paint captions on canvas still won’t work.

Languages

The top line shows subtitles in the language you're learning; click its words for definitions. The second line shows your language: the video's own subtitles when available, otherwise YouTube's auto-translation. "Auto" uses the language spoken in the video.

OpenSubtitles

Optional fallback for movie sites when the player has no captions. Search by film or series title from the Script panel. Get a free API key at opensubtitles.com/consumers — your key stays in this browser.

AI explanations

Optional. Your key stays in this browser and is sent only to the provider you choose. With a key, phrases and words without a dictionary entry are explained in context, in your language. When you save or test a key, Chrome asks for permission to contact that provider.
